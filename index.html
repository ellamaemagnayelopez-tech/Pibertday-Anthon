Happy birthday, Byyy. ♡

Nine years.

Grabe, no? Parang ang bilis pero kapag binalikan ko lahat ng nangyari sa atin, ang dami na rin pala nating pinagdaanan together.

From those little moments noong nagsisimula pa lang tayo, hanggang sa mga trips, random dates, tawanan, tampuhan, at lahat ng ordinary days na somehow naging special dahil kasama kita.

Hindi naman naging perfect lahat. May times na nag-aaway tayo, may times na hindi tayo nagkakaintindihan, at may mga araw na pareho tayong nakakainis. 😂

Pero kahit ganon, ikaw pa rin yung taong gusto kong kasama pagkatapos ng lahat.

I’m really proud of you, Byyy.

I’ve watched you grow from the person I first met into the person you are today. From being a student, to becoming a graduate, passing your REE, becoming an LPT, teaching, and now working on your Master's.

Ang dami mong na-achieve, and I hope you know na sobrang proud ako sa'yo.

And yes, kahit ang dami mong achievements...

bad ka pa rin sa gawaing bahay. 😂

But seriously, thank you.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for growing with me. Thank you for all the memories, the laughs, the random conversations, the trips, the food dates, and even the little moments na hindi natin akalain na magiging memories pala someday.

You became such a big part of my life that sometimes I can't even imagine what my life would look like without all our memories in it.

And after nine years, you're still my favorite person to annoy.

My favorite person to travel with.

My favorite person to make memories with.

And, unfortunately for you, my favorite person to love. 😂♡

I don't know exactly what the next chapters will look like.

But I hope we get to keep writing them together.

More places to visit.
More food to try.
More random pictures.
More birthdays.
More celebrations.
More "saan tayo kakain?" moments. 😂
More ordinary days that somehow become our favorite memories.

So today, I just want you to know this:

I am so, so proud of you.

And I'm grateful that after all these years, I still get to call you my Byyy.

Happy 27th birthday, Anthon.

I love you.
Always have.
Always will. ♡
